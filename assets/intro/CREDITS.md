# Intro slide photography

All three are Unsplash, under the Unsplash Licence: free for commercial use,
no attribution required. Credited here anyway, so the provenance of anything
shipped in the app is recorded rather than remembered.

Each was downloaded at 2000px, cropped to 3:4 with the subject in the middle
band, and re-encoded at 1200x1600 — the slot is 390x520 logical, so this
covers a 3x screen with a little to spare.

| Slide | Photographer | Original |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — open road | Brendan Church | https://unsplash.com/photos/empty-road-covered-with-fogs-HaLpgS9xOgo |
| 2 — morning gates | Miguel Alcântara | https://unsplash.com/photos/a-black-and-white-photo-of-a-long-hallway-aDcjYLgVRuI |
| 3 — high desert | Abdullah Ahmad | https://unsplash.com/photos/mountains-covered-with-fog-during-daytime--1KE16hMtNs |

Chosen against the brief in `intro-slide-image-prompts.txt`: subject in the
middle band, soft low-detail bottom so the photo dissolves into the canvas,
plain top strip for the Skip button, and nothing green anywhere — the palette
admits one accent, an acid lime, and a green in a photograph competes with it.
Checked: zero green pixels in all three.
